Version: 0.2.45
  Changes:
    - Income, Active Income and Expense stations are limited to $20,000 a month at any month length. The limit used to shrink with shorter months.
    - Plates still leave a station at belt speed, so a $20,000 station needs about 45 seconds to empty a blue belt; in shorter months leftover income plates carry over and unsent bills are added to debt.

Version: 0.2.44
  Features:
    - Station amounts, Active Income salary, Debt APR, Investment return, Expense Needs/Wants and the Account name can be edited while the Account runs. Amounts and rates apply from the next month.
  Changes:
    - Linking and unlinking a station, and the starting debt and assets, still require pausing the Account.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.43
  Gui:
    - The Account is a centered, draggable dialog closed by Esc, E or its X button, with a live dashboard: month progress, assets, debt, net worth, monthly cashflow and how much of the monthly expenses investment returns cover.
    - Start checklist: Start stays disabled until a Cashflow Station, Debt Station and Investment Account are linked. Starting debt and assets become locked text after the first Start.
    - Station panels close with the vanilla window they sit beside.
    - Number fields accept digits only and show a $ or % suffix. Validation errors appear inside the panel instead of chat.
    - Expense Needs/Wants is a switch. The account picker shows each account's position, and a Locate button prints a map link.
    - Each station panel has a collapsible "how to connect" section, remembered per player.
    - Yearly reports are a table.
  Features:
    - Port labels carry the iron or copper plate icon and show only in alt mode.
    - Passive Income and Expense labels show how many plates are left to send.
    - Closing a month floats a cash in / bills / paid summary above each Cashflow Station.
  Changes:
    - World labels and panels use the same station names.
    - Panel text moved to the locale file.
